How to play

Find the secret word. You have unlimited guesses.

The words are sorted by how similar they are to the secret word. After each guess you see its position: the secret word is #1, the lower the number the closer you are.

kitten42

  • Green: top 300 – very close.
  • Yellow: 300 to 1500.
  • Red: further away.

Similarity is about meaning and context, not spelling: words that show up in similar texts are close. It comes from word vectors (GloVe) learned from Wikipedia and news articles, so some neighbours may surprise you.

Plurals and verb forms count as their base word (cats → cat). Very common words like the or with don't count.

Stuck? The 💡 hint reveals a word closer than your best guess. There is a new word every day at midnight, and you can play all previous games.

Give up?

The secret word will be revealed and this game counts as not solved.
